Estructuras gramaticales avanzadas indispensables para Writing Task 2 y Speaking

📌 Inversion Structure (Estructuras Invertidas)

Se usan para enfatizar puntos clave en ensayos académicos. Requieren alterar el orden del sujeto y el auxiliar después de expresiones negativas/restrictivas como Not only, Seldom, Rarely, Under no circumstances.

EJERCICIO 1
"Not only [ _____ ] greenhouse gas emissions, but it also creates thousands of green jobs."
EJERCICIO 2 (REWRITE & REORDER)
Completa la inversión con el auxiliar correcto:
"Seldom [ _____ ] governments encounter such widespread public consensus."

📌 Mixed Conditionals (Condicionales Mixtos)

Conectan una condición del pasado con un resultado presente (o viceversa). Ideales para analizar causas históricas e impactos actuales en el Writing Task 2.

EJERCICIO 3
"If the council [ _____ ] in public transport years ago, the city would not face severe congestion today."
EJERCICIO 4
"If I were not deeply committed to environmental protection, I [ _____ ] for that NGO last year."

📌 Participle Clauses (Cláusulas Participiales)

Permiten condensar información y mejorar la cohesión eliminando conectores repetitivos como "because", "after", "and". Ejemplo: Having completed the study, researchers published the findings.

EJERCICIO 5
"[ _____ ] the long-term consequences of climate change, policymakers drafted a new environmental treaty."
EJERCICIO 6 (FILL IN THE BLANK)
"[ _____ ] by recent technological advances, traditional industries are adapting rapidly." (Usa la forma en participio pasado de DRIVE).

📌 Cleft Sentences (Estructuras de Énfasis)

Muestran un dominio gramatical Band 8.0+ al reorganizar la oración para destacar un elemento específico usando What... is/was o It is/was... that.

EJERCICIO 7
Transforma para dar énfasis: "The lack of funding causes the problem." → "What causes the problem [ _____ ] the lack of funding."
EJERCICIO 8
"It is through sustainable urban planning [ _____ ] modern cities can resolve housing shortages."

📌 Advanced Passive Structures (Estructuras Pasivas Impersonales)

Esenciales para un tono académico neutro e impersonal. Se forman con It is widely believed that... o X is thought to be...

EJERCICIO 9
"Automation [ _____ ] to transform the global labor market significantly over the coming decade."
EJERCICIO 10 (FILL IN THE BLANK)
"It is universally [ _____ ] that early childhood education yields lifelong benefits." (Usa el participio pasado de ACKNOWLEDGE).
